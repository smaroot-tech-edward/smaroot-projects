# Smaroot projects / 定制开发与产品工程

**Business workflows, interfaces and engineering decisions you can inspect.**

Smaroot (慧根智研) builds custom business software and its own automation products. This collection explains the problem, user journey, delivered scope and relevant engineering considerations behind our work.

## Customer delivery cases / 客户定制交付

| Project | Business journey | What to inspect |
|---|---|---|
| [Community & assisted matching mini program](community-service.md) | Profile → discovery → interaction → conversation → human-assisted service | Mini program, backend, moderation and administrator audit |
| [Vehicle battery service mini program](battery-service.md) | Customer/vehicle record → agreement → deposit record → installation → service term → return | A continuous service lifecycle and clear customer-facing status |

These two cases are published as **authorized, anonymized customer deliveries** in Smaroot's public case library. Customer identities, private records and commercial metrics are not included. Each page links to its source and explains the scope of its images.
